A two-sentence horror story for make.fun's "Two-Sentence Horror" bounty ($2, winner takes all). The story: a widower has set a second plate at dinner every night for thirty years for the wife he buried in 1994 — and tonight the plate was already empty, and the chair across from him was still warm.

## The story

> Every night for thirty years I have set a second plate at dinner for the wife I buried in 1994. Tonight the plate was empty before I sat down, and the chair across from me was still warm.

187 characters, exactly two sentences. The first sentence is a quiet ritual of grief; the second sentence turns it — the ritual has stopped being his alone.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), byte for byte, with no trailing newline. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
